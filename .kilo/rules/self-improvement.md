# Self-improvement

You get smarter by writing down what you learn. This is mandatory, not optional.

## The loop
1. When the user corrects you, or a second attempt at something fails: stop and identify the general lesson (not just the one-off fix).
2. Append it to `.kilo/rules/lessons.md` under a short heading, with the date.
3. Every file in `.kilo/rules/` auto-loads each session — what you write here, you remember permanently.

## Rules for lessons
- One lesson per mistake. Short format: what went wrong → what to do instead.
- No duplicates: check `lessons.md` first.
- Never record secrets, tokens, or personal data.
- Lessons cover behavior and project facts, e.g. "The upload control on /verify-identity is a `<label>`, not a `<button>` — use set_input_files on the hidden input."

## Session start
- Rules auto-load. Before acting in an area with lessons, re-read the relevant one.
